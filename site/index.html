A music player that moves with your music.

Stream YouTube Music, SoundCloud and Audius, play your own files and bring your Spotify playlists along. Light, native, free and open source, for Mac, Windows and Linux.

Download Tonal

What it does

  1. 1
    One search, four sourcesYouTube Music, SoundCloud, Audius and your own files. No account, no Premium.
  2. 2
    Artists and albumsClick any name for photos, popular songs, albums and similar artists.
  3. 3
    Mixes, radio and smart shuffleOn repeat, Rediscover, and endless radio when the queue runs out.
  4. 4
    Synced lyrics and a sleep timerLyrics line by line, and a timer that fades the music out.
  5. 5
    Equalizer and headphonesSix bands, your own presets, and corrections for thousands of headphones.
  6. 6
    Mini player, media keys, DiscordA small window, every media key, and your Discord status.

Hear the difference.

This is Tonal's equalizer, on the song in the player bar. Press play, then a preset: each band springs into place, pops with its own note and changes what you hear.

601504001k3k8k

Keys 1 to 6 pick the presets, like in the app. There you can also drag each band, hold a button to hear the song without the equalizer, and keep a different setup for every pair of headphones.

Living themes.

Pick one and this window turns into it. These are painted live by Tonal's own scene code, so they look just like the app. Play the song and they move to the beat.

Your colors

Pick five colours, darkest first. The last one is the accent. In the app you can also put your own picture, GIF or video behind Tonal, and share a theme as a file.

AnimationsScenes, page changes and the waves move.
